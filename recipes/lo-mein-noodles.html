Lo Mein Noodles

Home

Ingredients

  • 1 (8 ounce) package spaghetti
  • 3 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons teriyaki sauce
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 3 stalks celery, sliced
  • 2 large carrots, cut into large matchsticks
  • 1/2 sweet onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 green onions, sliced plus more for garnish

Directions

  1. Step 1: Gather all ingredients.
  2. Step 2: Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Cook spaghetti in boiling water, stirring occasionally, until tender yet firm to the bite, about 12 minutes; drain, then rinse with cold water to cool.
  3. Step 3: Meanwhile, whisk soy sauce, teriyaki saunce, honey, and ginger together in a small bowl; set aside.
  4. Step 4: While the spaghetti cooks, heat oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat. Cook and stir celery, carrots, onion, and green onion unitl slightly tender, 5 to 7 minutes.
  5. Step 5: Add spaghetti and soy sauce mixture. Cook, stirring frequently, until heated through and evenly coated, about 2 to 3 minutes. Serve garnished with additional sliced green onions, if desired.
