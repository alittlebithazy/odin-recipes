Beef and Noodles

Home

Ingredients

  • 1 pound cubed beef stew meat
  • 1 cup chopped onion
  • 1 cup chopped celery
  • 1 cup chopped carrots
  • 1/4 cup beef bouillon granules
  • 1/4 teaspoon dried parsley
  • 1 pinch ground black pepper
  • 5 3/4 cups water
  • 2 1/2 cups frozen egg noodles
  1. Step 1: Saute stew meat, onion, and celery in a large saucapan over medium-high heat until meat is browned on all sides, about 5 minutes.
  2. Step 2: Stir in carrots, then season with bouillon, parsley, and pepper. Stir in water and add egg noodles.
  3. Step 3: Bring to a boil, reduce heat to low, and simmer for 30 minutes.
